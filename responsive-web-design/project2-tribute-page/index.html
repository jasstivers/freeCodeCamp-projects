The Tribute to Grace Hopper

The woman who revolutionized programming languages

Here's a timeline of Grace Hopper's life:

  • 1906 - Born in New York City, USA
  • 1934 - Graduates from Yale University with a PhD in Mathematics
  • 1943 - Joins the U.S. Navy during World War II and is assigned to the Harvard University Computation Laboratory
  • 1952 - Develops the first compiler, known as the A-0 system, which translates human-readable instructions into machine code
  • 1959 - Invents the concept of a "compiler" that led to the development of the COBOL programming language
  • 1969 - Retires from the U.S. Navy with the rank of Rear Admiral
  • 1986 - Becomes the first woman to receive the National Medal of Technology
  • 1992 - Passes away at the age of 85
"Grace Hopper's work changed the way we think about computers and programming, and she remains an inspiration to generations of computer scientists."
-- Computer Scientist
